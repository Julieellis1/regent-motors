FAQ

MOT questions

When is my MOT due?

Check your last MOT certificate, or use the free GOV.UK MOT history checker.

Can I MOT early?

Yes. You can have it tested up to a month (minus a day) before it expires and keep your renewal date.

How long does it take?

Usually around 45–60 minutes.

Do you test motorcycles?

Yes, we test Classes 1 and 2.

Can you collect my car?

Yes, collection & delivery is available. Just ask.
